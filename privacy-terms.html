Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use Jaba Shop, we collect the following information to provide our services:

  • Account Information: Email address and password when you register.
  • Payment Receipts: Screenshots of payment confirmations uploaded for purchase verification.
  • Usage Data: Basic analytics about how you interact with our platform (pages visited, courses viewed).
  • Device Information: Browser type, IP address, and device type for security and optimization.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data strictly for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your account and digital library.
  • To verify payment receipts and grant access to purchased courses.
  • To communicate with you about your purchases, account status, or support requests.
  • To improve our platform and develop new educational content.
  • To detect and prevent fraud or unauthorized access.

3. Data Security

Your security is our priority. We implement industry-standard measures to protect your data:

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS encrypted connections.
  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.
  • Payment receipts are stored in secure cloud storage with restricted access.
  • We never store your Telebirr or bank account details — payments are handled externally.

4. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to power our platform:

  • Supabase — for authentication, database, and secure file storage.
  • Telebirr / Awash Bank — for payment processing (we only see your receipt, not your account).

These services have their own privacy policies and are bound by strict data protection standards.

5. Your Rights

You have full control over your personal data:

  • Access: View all data associated with your account in your dashboard.
  • Correction: Update your email or profile information anytime.
  • Deletion: Request complete account deletion from your profile settings. All data is permanently removed within 30 days.
  • Portability: Download your purchased courses at any time from your library.

6. Cookies & Tracking

We use minimal cookies only for essential functions: keeping you logged in and remembering your preferences. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising or sell your browsing data to third parties.

7. Children's Privacy

Jaba Shop is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
