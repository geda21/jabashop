Privacy & Security

Your Data is Safe With Us

Learn how we protect your information and keep your learning experience secure

Data Protection

Your personal information is encrypted and stored securely. We never share your data with third parties. All payment receipts are stored with restricted access, visible only to our admin team for verification purposes.

Payment Security

We don't store your payment details. All financial transactions are handled directly through Telebirr or your bank. Receipts are uploaded via secure, encrypted connections and automatically processed for approval.

Account Privacy

Your account information, purchase history, and library are private to you. Only you can access your dashboard. We recommend using a strong, unique password for your account.

Content Ownership

All digital products purchased are for personal use only. Redistribution, resale, or sharing of our content is strictly prohibited and violates our terms of service.

Data Deletion

If you delete your account, all your personal data and purchase records are permanently removed from our system within 30 days. You can request account deletion from your profile settings.
